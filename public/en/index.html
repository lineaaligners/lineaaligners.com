FAQ

Can I do the treatment if I live in the UK or USA?

Yes. The 3D scan is done in Pejë during your visit (or you send us a scan from your dentist), and we follow your treatment remotely via the Linea portal and WhatsApp.

How long do I need to stay in Kosovo?

The scan takes minutes. Aligners are ready within 48 hours after you approve your 3D plan, so a few days is usually enough.

How does remote follow-up work?

The Linea portal reminds you when to switch aligners and lets you upload progress photos. Questions go straight to us on WhatsApp.

What if a refinement is needed?

A new scan is taken, on your next visit to Pejë or at a dentist where you live, and we adjust the plan.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your case. After the scan you get an exact written quote, with 0% interest installments available.
